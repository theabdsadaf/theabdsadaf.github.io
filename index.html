About

Born on May 20, 2004, in Narayanganj, Bangladesh, Sadaf Abdullah has grown into a driven and disciplined individual with a strong passion for technology, problem-solving, and continuous self-improvement. He currently lives in Bashundhara Residential Area, Dhaka, and is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University.

His technical foundation rests on competitive programming, algorithms and data structures, practical web development, and an increasing focus on machine learning. Alongside formal study he has taught students, which continues to shape how he explains and structures knowledge. He is gradually orienting toward academic research while remaining honest about being at the beginning of that path.
